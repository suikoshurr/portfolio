The outcome — a second life.

MODULE 08 / WHERE TABLES LANDED — 2023 → TODAY

Tables still lives in the project container — but the market voted on what it was for. At launch it reached just 12% of the existing customer base. The honest read: customers didn't need a general-purpose database; they needed one core job done well — tracking to-do's inside internal teams. So we repurposed it: the old to-do's were replaced with the data-powered table, shipped with pre-configured templates like expenditure tracking. That pivot changed everything.

WHAT TABLE DOES NOW — ONE JOB, DONE WELL
O-01 · THE PIVOT
Data-powered to-do's

The old to-do's were swapped for the table engine, with pre-configured templates — expenditure tracking and more — so teams start from something useful, not a blank grid.

O-02 · INTERNAL ONLY
A manager's back-office

Team managers track and assign work through tables — fully internal. Clients and other external parties never see it, keeping the working layer separate from the client-facing one.

O-03 · STILL GROWING
Comments on to-do's

Comments now live inside to-do's — conversation happens where the work is, and it's driving even more collaboration through tables.

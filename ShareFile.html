MODULE 02 / SITUATION ON ARRIVAL

After the platform-heavy world of Innovaccer, I landed at ShareFile at a moment of total reset — the product was separating from Citrix and redefining itself as an independent business. I came in as the second India-based product designer, handed a blank slate called "Projects" — a brand-new vertical inside a product with serious scale. No patterns to lean on. No safety net of precedent. 90,000 customers who couldn't feel a bump.

MODULE 04 / WHO USES SHAREFILE — WHO I DESIGN FOR
P-01 · RUNS THE FIRM
Service providers

Owners of tax consultancies, law firms, real-estate and insurance practices. Small-to-medium teams overseeing client projects — coordinating people inside and outside ShareFile.

P-02 · RUNS THE WORK
Associates

Small teams collecting documents, information and signatures — reviewing and chasing until submissions are complete. Their pains: turnaround time, usability, unorganised data across clients.

P-03 · FEELS THE WORK
Clients

The person on the other side — clients of our customers. Uploading files, signing documents, entering structured information on time, while trying to stay aware of the overall progress of their service.

MODULE 05 / FEATURES & SYSTEMS I'VE TOUCHED
C-01
Projects

A container that's timebound, configurable, and holds the context of the whole work lifecycle.

C-02
Make projects rich

Functionality that earns its place in work management — collecting information, storing it, and a communication layer on top.

C-03
Make resources reusable

Templates that sell on the marketplace — creators publish them, organisations enable them for everyday use.

C-04
Maturity

So many moving parts in one container creates a scalability problem — making it all feel seamless was the real challenge.

Integrate

Projects as a connected experience — pulling relevant information across projects, storing artifacts back into customers' existing file and folder structures.

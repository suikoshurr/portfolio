MODULE 02 / SITUATION ON ARRIVAL

After my stint at Lollypop Design Studio, moving from service-based work to a full-fledged product team, Innovaccer was my stepping stone into technical development. The company was scaling fast — and with that came scalability issues: leaking security and breaking application shells. As it moved to a multi-tenant micro-architecture, security demanded a more robust, controlled access system and integration with third-party auth providers working inside consumers' security layers. My role grew into platform and system design — my work was cross-platform, in constant collaboration with other teams.

MODULE 04 / WHO USES INNOVACCER — WHO I DESIGN FOR
P-01 · GOVERNS ACCESS
Admins

Control provisioning and de-provisioning across a hospital org — ensuring the right person gets the right type of access.

P-02 · PROVIDES CARE
Caregivers

Nurses, in simpler terms — performing care protocols as directed by clinicians, recording numbers in the system for clarity and documentation.

P-03 · DIAGNOSIS
Clinicians / Doctors

The main link in the chain — providing the best diagnosis with the help of historical care data. The only people with credentialed access to a patient's PHI.

P-04 · GETTING CURED
Patients

Receiving care at the end of the day — updates, billing and care protocols, all consolidated on their Innovaccer app.

MODULE 05 / FEATURES & SYSTEMS I'VE TOUCHED
C-01
Research the user base

Around user groups and permissions — what people create, and why they create it.

C-02
Map third-party auth

Research how customers' third-party auth systems are structured — and how ours must fit inside their security layers.

C-03
A robust role system

Reusable roles catering to at least 70% of user needs — without dependence on custom roles.

C-04
Measure usage

Instrument how roles and modules are actually used — so the system standardises around evidence, not assumption.

C-05
Global navigation system

Design a global navigation for the application that is adaptive and modular — one that scales with the pace of new modules entering the Innovaccer suite instead of breaking under it.
